The Sustainable Mobility Allowance (FMD) is a financial scheme set up by the government to encourage more sustainable commuting practices, such as cycling, carpooling, or public transit.

Employers can contribute up to €800 per year per employee, completely exempt from social charges and income tax.

Implementing the FMD through Highwages has no setup fees and requires zero fleet management. The financial contribution is entirely flexible and up to what the company chooses to allocate, while generating significant social charge exemptions.

All types of bikes are eligible—classic city bikes, electric bikes (e-bikes), cargo bikes, and folding bikes from our marketplace comprising over 3,000 top brand references.

The rollout is completely turnkey. We handle communication, provide access to our online marketplace, manage employer contributions, and coordinate direct home or partner shop delivery for employees.
