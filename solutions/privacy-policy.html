Who collects your data?

BEETOGREEN SAS, a simplified joint-stock company with capital of €30,000, registered with the Paris RCS under number 882 703 531 and whose head office is located at 47 Rue Voltaire 92300 Levallois-Perret France, provides a platform under the name 'BeeToGreen' accessible from the website it operates (www.beetogreen.com).

As part of the provision of its services in relation to the Platform, BEETOGREEN SAS is required to collect and process Personal Data concerning users, in order to enable it to manage the contractual relationship. In this context, BEETOGREEN SAS is required to set up data processing, in compliance with the GDPR, the purposes of which are the management of customers (including the management of contractual relations, orders, invoices, accounting), and more generally the management of operations allowing it to provide the functionalities necessary for navigation on our platform.

Data collected on the BeeToGreen platform

• Data entered directly by your company and by you, when creating your account or modifying your profile.

• Data that we collect automatically as a result of your actions on the platform.

Data entered by the company and the user

The mandatory or optional nature of the data is indicated to you at the time of collection: last name, first name, postal address, email address, telephone number.

Data filled in automatically

Certain data is collected automatically as a result of your actions on the site: navigation data, order history. We also carry out audience measurements: for example, we measure the number of page views, the number of visits to the site, as well as the activity of visitors to the site and their frequency of return. Finally, we collect data using cookies.

Certain personal data (namely last name, first name, postal address, email address, telephone number and for payment purposes your banking data by our payment providers Alma and Stripe) must be collected in order for you to benefit from our services. If this information is not completed, you cannot access the products on the BeeToGreen platform.

Bank data

For payment purposes, bank details are collected and stored by our PCI-DSS certified payment providers Alma and Stripe.

This standard is an international security standard whose objectives are to ensure the confidentiality and integrity of cardholder data, and thus secure the protection of card and transaction data. Alma and Stripe encrypt the data and only store it for technical purposes (for example: the reimbursement of a purchase, in the event of fraudulent behavior) for a period of 3 years. Encrypted payment data is stored in secure data centers in Europe.

Data retention rules

Your Personal Data concerning your account are kept during the subscription period to the BeeToGreen platform, contracted by your company. Those concerning your browsing and/or your behavior on the site are kept for a period of 13 months from your last activity on the site unless otherwise provided by law. At the end of this period, your account is considered 'inactive' and will be automatically deactivated.

However, at the end of these aforementioned deadlines, including as necessary from your request for deletion, your personal data may be subject to intermediate archiving in order to meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations (such as the obligation to store invoices for a period of 10 years as set out in Article L.123-22 of the Commercial Code) and/or at least during the applicable limitation period (such as the limitation period common law of 5 years enshrined in article 2224 of the Civil Code).

The data collected on the BeeToGreen site are exclusively intended for the BeeToGreen company. Under no circumstances will BeeToGreen transmit your personal data to third parties for other purposes such as commercial and/or promotional.
