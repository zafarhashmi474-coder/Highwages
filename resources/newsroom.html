Corporate cycling: the
market is growing, but
education remains
a major challenge

Provided their company has implemented it, the Sustainable Mobility Allowance enables any employee to finance their bike through employer support of up to €600 per year. Still relatively little known, this scheme is a powerful tool for decarbonising commuting, as explained by Antoine du Teilleul, co-founder of Beetogreen.
